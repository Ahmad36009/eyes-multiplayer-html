# Eyes Multiplayer HTML

An unofficial browser recreation of *Eyes: The Horror Game*. Explore a dark house, collect money bags, and make it back to the exit while Krasue hunts you. Use eye runes to see through her eyes and plan your route.

[Play in your browser](https://ahmad36009.github.io/eyes-multiplayer-html/)

- **Solo** — Escape the house on your own.
- **Co-op** — Share a room code, explore together, and collect money bags.
- **Versus** — One player controls Krasue while the others try to escape.

Use a keyboard and mouse on desktop or the on-screen controls on mobile.
