# Eyes Multiplayer HTML

An unofficial browser recreation of Eyes: The Horror Game 2.2. Explore a dark house, collect money bags, and find your way back to the exit while Krasue hunts you. Use eye runes to briefly see through her eyes and plan your next move.

[Play in your browser](https://ahmad36009.github.io/eyes-multiplayer-html/)

## Game modes

- **Solo:** Explore the house and escape on your own.
- **Co-op:** Share a room code and collect money bags with friends.
- **Versus:** One player controls Krasue while the survivors try to escape.

The game supports keyboard and mouse on desktop and touch controls on mobile.
